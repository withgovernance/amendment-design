Whose type, whose ground

One bill card (S. 1582, the GENIUS Act, live data) drawn four ways inside a conversation host. There are two separate choices. Type: Amendment’s Newsreader and Archivo, or the host’s sans. Ground: Amendment’s opaque canvas and inks, or the host’s background, text and border colours. Status, action and vote colours stay Amendment’s in every version; hosts publish none.

  1. A · Scope holds. Amendment type, Amendment ground.
  2. B · Recommended split. Amendment type, host ground.
  3. C · RATIFY option (c). Host type, Amendment ground.
  4. D · Today’s theming (#3518). Host type, host ground. Drawn on the redesign anatomy, not the live card’s.
  5. E · Ruled 2026-11-09. D with no ground of its own: the card is transparent and the conversation shows through, as Claude’s MCP Apps guidance asks. See RATIFY.md, “MCP App cards take the host’s fonts and colours”.

The host frame uses approximate Claude chat colours, not sampled values, and system-ui stands in for Claude’s sans. Judge the relationships, not the exact hexes. The nay fill is a placeholder (--text-tertiary); its token is open in RATIFY.md.
