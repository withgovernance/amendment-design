Email is a viewport, not a canvas

The same role the browser window plays on the web: objects are placed into it. So paper is not email’s background — paper appears only where a paper artifact appears, and one message can hold two registers. A profile is an existing register plus a substitution table; it is not a fifth register.

A webfont reaches a minority of clients while blocking render in the rest. Verdana is the widest email-safe sans, so the chrome register stays wide — and with no axis to expand the face, shrinking it is the one move that has nothing left to give.
