The measure

A measure caps the element that holds the text, and it is stated in rem derived from a counted line. The count is the intent; the rem is the value.

was: 65ch on the page shell636px · … characters

I rent in your district and my lease went up eleven percent this spring, which is the third increase in four years and more than my pay has moved in that time.

measure.body · 28rem448px · … characters

I rent in your district and my lease went up eleven percent this spring, which is the third increase in four years and more than my pay has moved in that time.

measure.lede · 24rem384px · … characters

Your landlord could raise the rent by any amount, with sixty days’ notice.

The unit
ch is banned from length tokens. It is the advance width of the zero glyph of the element carrying the rule — so 65ch on a shell that inherits the body sans, wrapping serif prose, rendered 97 characters. Nobody chose 97.
Two ambiguities
Zero vs average glyph within a face is 42% in Newsreader; measuring face vs rendered face between two is 50%. Self-consistency fixes only the second: 65ch set on the serif itself is still 92 characters.
Where it goes
On the text, not the shell. A page may be wide; the paragraph is capped. container.* are shells, measure.* are measures.
The counts
Target 69, ceiling 75. measure.body min(28rem, 100% - 2rem); measure.lede min(24rem, 100% - 2rem). The min() is why neither needs a breakpoint. Metadata grids, rolls and tallies are not copy and may run wider.
On paper
6.5in, set by the 1in margin — paper takes its measure from the margin, not from a count. See profilesPrint.
