Uppercase has two homes

The hard rule said uppercase belongs to chrome alone, while role-ceremonial sat in the stylesheet being defined by display-size uppercase. The exception was a whole role, which is why the rule read as absolute for so long.

Designation or sentence?

The test, because "display-size uppercase" is not self-evidently one or the other. A designation is a name assigned to a thing and carried on the artifact. A sentence is something a person wrote. Setting a sentence in tracked uppercase does not make it ceremonial — it makes it hard to read at the size that was supposed to make it grand.

This is what the role layer is for. A role is not a convenience or a shorthand — it is the only form in which a register is checkable. A hand-assembled register has no name, so nothing can assert about it: no lint rule, no test, no review. Give it a name and one grep decides it. Which is why the rule that catches this greps the assembly — sans-chrome adjacent to uppercase without role-label-caps — rather than measuring the value. The assembly is greppable and the value is not, and the rule found the input-label utility, where one line put every labelled field in the product five times under and no call site was wrong.
