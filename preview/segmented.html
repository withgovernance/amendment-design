No tier reaches 1.15:1 in either scheme, against the 3:1 a non-text signal owes. Sampled off this card's own render at its own position — a different ground, so a different number, per the rule — the shipped pair measures 1.11 light and 1.08 dark. The step is visible; it is nowhere near carrying a state on its own. And under inversion it runs backwards: every dark tier is a black overlay, so the raised plate paints darker (11,12,16) than the trough around it (19,22,28). Whatever reading A appears to be doing, it is doing with the ink step it also happens to have.

Geometry and motion

Each segment declares min-height: 44px, per a11y.targetSize — the track's height is then 52px, which is 44 plus the track's 4px gutter top and bottom, not a number anyone picked. The gutter is 4px because a radii.sm chip plus 4px is exactly a radii.md track — concentric, and both values on the scale. Two or three segments; past three this is a select. Motion is a background cross-fade and nothing else: a plate that translates is components.toggle's signal, and the two controls must not read alike.
