Aurora, flat

assets/aurora-still.svg — the three-stop Blue Hour baked to a static file, for every transport that cannot run a live gradient stack.

Where it runs
Email (no backdrop-filter, no layered radials), raster export (the Receipt's three ratios), and anywhere a single <img> is the only thing that survives the transport.
Where it does not
Print. The aurora cannot exist on paper and never carried meaning — see profilesPrint. This asset is not the print fallback; there is no print aurora at all.
