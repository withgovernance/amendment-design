The submit is the one control the system already has an answer for

Above, the submit is button-cta: --color-action at full strength carrying --color-action-label, flat, 48px declared. Below is what ships — a near-black slab in light and a light glass slab under inversion. Those are not one control in two schemes; they are two materials, and neither is the colour color.action is specified to carry every action and every act of assent.
