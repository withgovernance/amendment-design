HB 21 · Colorado · 2026

Concerning local authority to enact rent stabilization

If you rent in Colorado, this bill would let your city cap how much your rent can rise each year — something cities are barred from doing today.

Affects renters in home-rule municipalities — about 1 in 3 Colorado households.
It is in committee. A small group of legislators decides on May 28 whether it goes to a full vote.
Rep. Bensky represents you. She has not said how she will vote.
