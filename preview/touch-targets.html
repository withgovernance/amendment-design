The number
44 is the floor of three authorities, not a target. Apple HIG 44pt, WCAG 2.5.5 (AAA) 44 CSS px, Android 48dp. Two authorities and two unit systems arriving at one number is the argument for holding it. WCAG 2.2's newer 2.5.8 says 24px at AA — that is not an update to this, it is the number someone will cite to shrink a control.
Why declare it
40px is not a decision anybody made. It is padding + line-height, and it is what four controls here arrive at — the same way the tab bar shipped 40. The two that comply are the two that state a size.
How
min-height, not more padding. Padding changes the plate's proportions and the label's optical centring; a minimum leaves a compliant control alone and grows only what falls short.
When the control
must stay small
Expand the target, not the box. A transparent hit area at the minimum, centred — the appearance and the target are two different geometries, and this rule governs only the second. A 44px-tall toggle track stops reading as a switch.
Where 48
Dense, consequential, or thumb-reached: Send, Co-sign, the FAB, the tab bar.
